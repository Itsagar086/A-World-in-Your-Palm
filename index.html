WASD or arrow keys to walk. Shift to run. Space to hop. E to interact with a nearby landmark. Click or tap the ground to walk there. Drag to rotate the camera. Scroll or pinch to zoom. M toggles globe and follow views. N changes the time of day. P enters photo mode. J opens your field journal. Home returns to Banyan Hollow. Escape cancels your route. On touch screens, use the movement joystick and jump button.
